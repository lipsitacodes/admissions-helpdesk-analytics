Application Deadlines

The most important deadlines include the initial application date, the fee payment window, and the final submission deadline. Each academic term has a published calendar with exact dates.

Fee deadlines are separate from admission deadlines. Students should pay tuition and hostel deposits by the payment deadline to avoid late fees.

Scholarship applications may follow a different deadline schedule. Applicants should check the scholarship portal for eligibility and supporting document deadlines.

Students who miss a deadline should contact the admissions office immediately. Some deadlines may allow a short grace period, but late submission can affect enrollment status.
