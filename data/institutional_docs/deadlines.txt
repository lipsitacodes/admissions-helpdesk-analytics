Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Important Dates and Admission Deadlines (Academic Year 2025-2026 / 2026)

CUEE AND ADMISSION TIMELINES:
- CUEE Online Registration: Open on the CUTM admissions portal (admissions.cutm.ac.in / www.cutm.ac.in).
- Entrance Examination Schedule: CUEE tests are scheduled phase-wise. Specific test dates and time slots are published on the website and sent to candidates' registered email/phone numbers approximately 1 week prior to the examination.
- Provisional Admission Window: Qualified candidates are notified immediately after results and must deposit the provisional admission fee before the specified deadline to secure their seat.
- Document Verification & Final Registration: Dates for submitting original documents and photocopies are communicated in the provisional admission letter prior to commencement of classes.
- Academic Calendar & Semester Commencement: The official academic calendar detailing orientation, course commencement, mid-semester, and end-semester examinations is published by the Registrar's Office.
