Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Required Documents for Admission Verification (2025-2026 / 2026)

DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS FOR FINAL ADMISSION:
Applicants reporting for final admission verification after CUEE qualification and provisional admission must submit the Original and 4 Sets of Photocopies of the following documents:

1. Qualifying Examination Marksheet:
   - Class 10th / Matriculation Marksheet & Certificate (proof of date of birth).
   - Class 12th / +2 Science / Intermediate Marksheet & Pass Certificate (for UG programmes).
   - Graduation Degree Marksheets & Certificate (for PG programmes such as MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Sc).
   - Diploma Marksheet & Certificate (for Lateral Entry applicants).

2. Entrance Examination Credentials:
   - CUEE (Centurion University Entrance Examination) Rank Card / Score Card / Confirmation Letter.
   - National Entrance Score Card (JEE Main, OJEE, CAT, MAT, etc., if applying under merit/scholarship quotas).

3. Identity & Address Proof:
   - Aadhaar Card copy (mandatory).
   - PAN Card copy.

4. Administrative & Verification Records:
   - Provisional Admission Letter / Seat Allotment Letter.
   - Fee Payment Receipt / Transaction Confirmation.
   - Recent Passport-size Photographs (minimum 4-6 copies).
   - Transfer Certificate (TC) / College Leaving Certificate (CLC) / Migration Certificate.
   - Conduct / Character Certificate from the institution last attended.

5. Conditional / Category Certificates (if applicable):
   - Category / Caste Certificate (SC, ST, OBC, EWS) for claiming relaxation or reservation.
   - Income Certificate (for need-based / state scholarship processing).
   - Domicile / Resident Certificate.
   - Sports / Defence / Staff Ward supporting certificates (for Bhoomiputra, Sports, or Staff scholarships).
