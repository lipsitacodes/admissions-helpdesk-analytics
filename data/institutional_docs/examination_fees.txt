Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Examination Fee Structure and Policies (Academic Year 2025-2026 / 2026)

EXAMINATION FEE SCHEDULE:
1. Graduate and Postgraduate (PG) Courses (All Campuses):
   - Annual Examination Fee: INR 7,000 per year
   - Applicable to: B.Tech (including B.Tech CSE, CSE AIML, Mechanical, Civil, ECE, EEE, Aerospace, Biotech), MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Sc, B.Pharm, M.Pharm, B.Sc, BBA, B.Com, and all other Graduate and PG programmes across Bhubaneswar, Paralakhemundi, and other campuses.
   - Note: This examination fee of INR 7,000 is included as a line-item in the First Year Other Fee breakup (Total INR 25,000) and is charged for examination management, continuous assessment administration, and semester end examinations.

2. Diploma Courses (All Campuses):
   - Annual Examination Fee: INR 3,000 per year
   - Included in the Diploma First Year Other Fee breakup (Total INR 12,500).

3. D-Pharma (Bhubaneswar Campus):
   - Examination Fee: INR 7,000 per year (included in D-Pharma first-year other fee of INR 20,500).

CRITICAL DISTINCTION:
- The examination fee (INR 7,000 per year for Graduate/PG courses) is completely separate from the Academic Tuition Fee (for example, B.Tech CSE tuition fee is INR 185,000/yr at Bhubaneswar and INR 150,000/yr at Paralakhemundi).
- The examination fee covers semester examination administration, question paper setting, digital evaluation, and grade sheet generation.

EXAMINATION PROCEDURES & FORMS:
- Students must complete semester registration and examination form submission within the notified academic deadlines.
- Backlog, supplementary, and special examination schedules along with applicable per-paper fees are notified separately by the university Examination Cell before each semester examination cycle.
