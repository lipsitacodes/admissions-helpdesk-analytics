Examination and Academic Fees

IMPORTANT: Every amount in this document is SYNTHETIC_DEMO data created for project demonstration. It is not an official fee notice.

Synthetic examination fee is INR 3,000 per semester or INR 6,000 per academic year, separate from tuition. Practical examination processing is included in this demo amount.

Synthetic backlog or supplementary examination fee is INR 1,500 per paper. Synthetic examination form processing is INR 300 per form. Revaluation and other special examination charges are not modeled here.

Demo examination workflow: confirm registered subjects, complete the examination form through the official student channel, pay the synthetic examination charge, check the timetable, and keep the acknowledgement. Exact official form dates remain unavailable.

Students should use the current official examination notice for real amounts, deadlines, revaluation rules, supplementary rules, and payment instructions.
