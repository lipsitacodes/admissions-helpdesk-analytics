Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Academic Structure, Evaluation, and Attendance Guidelines

PROGRAMME DURATION AND SEMESTER STRUCTURE:
- B.Tech degree programmes are conducted over 4 academic years divided into 8 semesters.
- M.Tech, MBA, and MCA programmes are conducted over 2 academic years (4 semesters).
- BCA and BBA are conducted over 4 academic years.
- Each semester comprises classroom instruction, interactive tutorials, hands-on laboratory workshops, skill-track certifications, and industry internships.

ASSESSMENT AND EVALUATION SYSTEM:
- Student performance is evaluated through continuous internal assessment (quizzes, assignments, laboratory evaluations, and mid-term assessments) combined with comprehensive end-semester examinations.
- Grading follows a Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) awarding Letter Grades and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) on a 10-point scale.

ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT AND SCHOLARSHIP ELIGIBILITY:
- Students are required to maintain a minimum of 80% class attendance across all enrolled subjects to be eligible to sit for semester examinations without condonation.
- Maintaining at least 80% attendance and scoring a minimum 8.5 CGPA in semester exams is mandatory to receive the 5% scholarship continuation on Academic Fee from the second year onwards.
