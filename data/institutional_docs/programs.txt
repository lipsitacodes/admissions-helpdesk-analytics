Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Undergraduate and Postgraduate Academic Programmes (2025-2026 / 2026)

SCHOOL OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY:
- B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): 4-year undergraduate programme covering programming, algorithms, databases, operating systems, networking, software engineering, and core computing foundations. Offered at Bhubaneswar (INR 185,000/yr) and Paralakhemundi (INR 150,000/yr).
- B.Tech CSE with AI & ML (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning): 4-year undergraduate programme combining computing core with machine learning, deep learning, statistical modeling, and intelligent systems. Offered at Bhubaneswar (INR 200,000/yr) and Paralakhemundi (INR 165,000/yr).
- B.Tech Cyber Security: 4-year programme focused on network defense, digital forensics, ethical hacking, cryptography, and information security at Bhubaneswar (INR 185,000/yr).
- B.Tech Aerospace Engineering: 4-year programme covering aerodynamics, aerospace structures, propulsion, flight mechanics, and space systems at Bhubaneswar (INR 160,000/yr).
- B.Tech Biotechnology: 4-year programme covering bioprocess engineering, bioinformatics, molecular biology, and industrial biotechnology at Bhubaneswar (INR 165,000/yr).
- B.Tech Mechanical Engineering: 4-year programme covering thermodynamics, mechanics, manufacturing, CAD/CAM, and automotive engineering at Bhubaneswar (INR 155,000/yr) and Paralakhemundi (INR 110,000/yr).
- B.Tech Civil Engineering: 4-year programme covering structures, hydraulics, geotechnical engineering, surveying, and environmental infrastructure at Bhubaneswar (INR 155,000/yr) and Paralakhemundi (INR 110,000/yr).
- B.Tech Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE): 4-year programme covering power systems, control systems, electrical machines, and electronics at Bhubaneswar (INR 155,000/yr) and Paralakhemundi (INR 110,000/yr).
- B.Tech Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE): 4-year programme covering digital signal processing, VLSI, microcontrollers, and communication systems at Bhubaneswar (INR 155,000/yr) and Paralakhemundi (INR 120,000/yr).
- B.Tech Mining Engineering: 4-year programme at Bhubaneswar (INR 155,000/yr).
- B.Tech Dairy Technology / Agricultural Engineering / Phytopharmaceuticals: 4-year specialised programmes offered at Paralakhemundi (INR 110,000/yr).
- M.Tech: 2-year postgraduate programme at Bhubaneswar (INR 90,000/yr) and Paralakhemundi (INR 85,000/yr).
- MCA: 2-year postgraduate computer applications programme.
- BCA: 4-year undergraduate computer applications programme.

SCHOOL OF MANAGEMENT & COMMERCE:
- MBA: 2-year full-time programme at Bhubaneswar (INR 240,000/yr) and MBA Agri Business Management at Paralakhemundi (INR 700,000).
- BBA / B.Com: Undergraduate commerce and management programmes.

M.S. SWAMINATHAN SCHOOL OF AGRICULTURE & ALLIED DISCIPLINES:
- B.Sc (Hons) Agriculture: 4-year programme at Paralakhemundi (INR 150,000/yr).
- B.F.Sc (Bachelor of Fishery Science): 4-year programme at Paralakhemundi (INR 125,000/yr).
- B.V.Sc & A.H (Veterinary Science): 5-year programme at Paralakhemundi (INR 750,000/yr).
