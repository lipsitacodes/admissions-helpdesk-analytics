Eligibility Requirements

Eligibility depends on academic performance, age, and completion of prerequisite courses. Applicants must meet the minimum grade thresholds listed for their chosen program.

Required documents include transcripts, proof of identity, and any program-specific certifications. International students may also need to provide language test scores.

Some programs require additional entrance exams or portfolio submissions. Applicants should review the program-specific checklist before submitting their application.

The eligibility review process confirms that all materials are complete. If documents are missing or incomplete, the admissions team requests the missing information before making a final decision.
