Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Eligibility Criteria for Academic Year 2025-2026 / 2026

ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY PROGRAMMES:
- B.Tech (Mechanical, EEE, ECE, Civil, CSE, Aerospace, Mining):
  10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with minimum 50% aggregate marks (5% relaxation for reserved category candidates, i.e., 45%). CUEE score required.
- B.Tech (Agriculture Engineering, Dairy Technology, Phytopharmacy):
  10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology with minimum 50% marks at +2 level.
- Diploma Engineering:
  10th Pass (35%) and 12th (Physics, Chemistry, Maths) pass is eligible for lateral entry, along with CUEE score.
- Lateral Entry (LE) Scheme:
  Available for B.Tech (LE), Diploma (LE), B.Sc Radiology (LE), B.Sc MLT (LE), and B.Pharm (LE).

MANAGEMENT & APPLIED SCIENCES:
- B.Sc. IT: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.
- B.Sc. (Botany, Zoology, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics): 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology.
- B.Sc. Agriculture (B.Sc Ag): 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology — 60% from Odisha Board/CBSE/ICSE/Other boards, 80% for AP Board.
- BFSc (Bachelor of Fishery Science): 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology with minimum 50% marks.
- BBA, B.Com, BA: 10+2 pass in any stream.
- MBA: Graduation in any discipline + Entrance examination score (CUEE / national / state entrance).
- MA: Graduation in any discipline.
- M.Sc. (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics): B.Sc in respective disciplines (pass).
- M.Sc. (Zoology and Botany): B.Sc in Zoology, Botany or Life Sciences (pass).
- M.Sc. Microbiology / MLT: B.Sc in Life Science, B.Sc in Biology, BAMS, BHMS, Biotechnology, or B.Pharma.

PHARMACY, ALLIED HEALTH & FORENSICS:
- B.Pharm: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology with minimum 45% marks.
- M.Pharm: B.Pharm degree examination from an Indian university approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) with not less than 55% aggregate marks.
- Diploma Pharmacy (D.Pharm): 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology/Bioscience.
- B.Sc. Allied Health: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology with minimum 50% marks.
- Diploma Allied Health: 10+2 in Science (for NIOS +2 from any stream).
- Certificate Courses in Allied Health (1-2 years): 10+2 in Science.
- Certified in Allied Health (6 months): 10th Standard pass.
- M.Sc. Allied Health: B.Sc. with Biology or any Life Science.
- B.Sc. Forensic Science: 10+2 or equivalent in Science (Physics/Chemistry/Biology/Maths) with at least 55% for General/OBC/EWS and 50% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
- M.Sc. Forensic Science: Bachelor's degree in Science / Forensic Science / Medicine / Engineering / Pharmacy / Dentistry / AYUSH with at least 55% (50% for SC/ST/PwD).
- M.Sc. Cyber Security and Digital Forensics: B.E./B.Tech in Engg/Tech OR B.Sc (IT/CS/Electronics) OR BCA OR B.Sc Forensic Science with minimum 55% (50% for SC/ST/PwD).
- B.V.Sc & A.H (Veterinary Science): 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology as per VCI guidelines.
