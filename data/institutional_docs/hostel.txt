Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Hostel Accommodation and Campus Living

HOSTEL FACILITIES AND AMENITIES:
- Separate hostel facilities are provided for male and female students across Bhubaneswar and Paralakhemundi campuses.
- Rooms are available in shared and single occupancy configurations, furnished with individual beds, study tables, chairs, and cupboards.
- Campus living amenities include 24/7 high-speed Wi-Fi, round-the-clock security and CCTV surveillance, medical emergency assistance, recreational lounges, indoor/outdoor sports facilities, and backup power supply.

MESS AND DINING CHARGES:
- Hostels feature hygienically managed dining halls serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian balanced meal plans.
- Mess charges and hostel room rent are billed separately from academic tuition fees.

APPLICATION AND ALLOTMENT PROCESS:
- Students can apply for hostel accommodation during or immediately following provisional admission confirmation.
- Room allocation is conducted on a first-come, first-served basis subject to room type availability.
- A refundable caution/security deposit is collected at the time of hostel room allotment.

HOSTEL RULES AND CODE OF CONDUCT:
- Residents must adhere to campus curfew hours, visitor regulations, anti-ragging mandates, and noise guidelines to foster a productive academic environment.
