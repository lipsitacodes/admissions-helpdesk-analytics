Hostel Accommodation

The university hostel offers single and shared rooms with standard furnishings, a common dining hall, laundry service, and internet access.

Hostel applications open after admission decisions are released. Students should submit the hostel form and pay the housing deposit by the deadline.

Room assignments are based on availability and application timing. Returning students may request room renewals while new students receive assignment details before move-in day.

Hostel residents must follow the code of conduct, which includes quiet hours, guest policies, and safety procedures.
