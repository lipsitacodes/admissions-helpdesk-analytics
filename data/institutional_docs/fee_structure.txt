IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION ON EXAMINATION FEE:
The INR 7,000 examination fee is a component of the first-year other fee structure and is NOT the academic/tuition fee for B.Tech CSE or any other degree programme. Academic programme fees (e.g. B.Tech CSE Bhubaneswar INR 185,000/yr, Paralakhemundi INR 150,000/yr) are distinct from examination and other charges.
