Scholarship Programs

The scholarship program includes merit-based awards, need-based grants, and support for students in specific fields of study.

Eligible students must submit a scholarship application, academic transcripts, and a short statement explaining their financial need or academic goals.

Scholarship amounts can cover tuition, hostel fees, or both. Awards are typically confirmed after the admission decision and are payable for one academic year at a time.

Students should monitor scholarship deadlines and submit all documents by the published date to remain eligible for review.
