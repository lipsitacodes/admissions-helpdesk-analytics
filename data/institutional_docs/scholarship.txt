Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Scholarship Schemes (Academic Year 2025-2026 / 2026)

AMRIT KAAL SCHOLARSHIP (MERIT-BASED):

1. B.Tech CSE (Bhubaneswar and Paralakhemundi Campuses):
   - 70% to 80% in 12th: 10% Merit Scholarship on First Year Fees
   - 80% to 90% in 12th: 15% Merit Scholarship on First Year Fees
   - National JEE Rank within 1 Lakh OR OJEE Rank within 5000 OR 91%+ in 12th: 20% Merit Scholarship on First Year Fees

2. B.Tech Non-CSE (Mechanical, Civil, EEE, ECE, Mining, Aerospace, Biotechnology, Dairy, Agri) (Bhubaneswar and Paralakhemundi):
   - 60% to 70% in 12th: 10% Merit Scholarship on First Year Fees
   - 70% to 80% in 12th: 15% Merit Scholarship on First Year Fees
   - National JEE Rank within 1 Lakh OR OJEE Rank within 5000 OR 91%+ in 12th: 20% Merit Scholarship on First Year Fees

3. Chandrika Scholarship for Girls (Bhubaneswar and Paralakhemundi):
   - B.Tech Non-CSE Only: 60% in 12th receives 10% First Year Fees; 70% in 12th receives 20% First Year Fees.
   - Allied Health (B.Sc Optometry, MLT, Microbiology & M.Sc Opto, MLT, Microbio): 60%+ receives 10%; 70%+ receives 20% First Year Fees.

4. Other Programmes (Bhubaneswar & Paralakhemundi):
   - B.Sc Allied Health (except BPT & Nursing): 60%-70% -> 10%; 70%-90% -> 15% First Year Fees.
   - M.Sc Applied Science / MCA (Bhubaneswar): 60%-70% -> 10%; 70%+ -> 15% First Year Fees.
   - MBA: 60%-70% -> 10%; 70%+ -> 15% First Year Fees.
   - LAW UG Programmes (Bhubaneswar): 60%-70% -> 10%; 70%-80% -> 15% First Year Fees.
   - Diploma Regular: 50%-60% -> 10%; 60%-70% -> 15% First Year Fees.
   - B.Sc Animation & Multimedia / B.Sc Bio Tech (Bhubaneswar): 60%-70% -> 10%; 70%-80% -> 15% First Year Fees.
   - B.Sc Agriculture (Paralakhemundi): 70%+ (Odisha/CBSE/Other) -> 10%; 80%+ (AP Board) -> 10% First Year Fees.

SPECIAL CATEGORY SCHOLARSHIPS (ALL YEARS):
- Bhoomiputra Scholarship (For Defence / State Police / Paramilitary staff kids - except Veterinary, B.Sc Nursing, BPT, B.Pharm): 20% Scholarship for All Years (No other scholarship combinable).
- State Level Sports: 25% Scholarship for All Years.
- National Level Sports: 40% Scholarship for All Years.
- Centurion Staff Kids: 20% Scholarship for All Years.
- Centurion Staff Relative / Reference: 10% Scholarship on First Year Fees.

SCHOLARSHIP TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
1. Total number of scholarships awarded is limited per programme.
2. Scholarship eligibility is strictly based on final scores in the last qualifying examination. No dual scholarships permitted.
3. Candidates must appear for and qualify CUEE (Centurion University Entrance Examination).
4. Mandatory verification of original scorecard by university admissions team.
5. Final scholarship eligibility list is published following formal approval by the University Scholarship Committee.

SECOND YEAR ONWARDS SCHOLARSHIP RENEWAL CRITERIA:
1. Student must score a minimum of 8.5 CGPA in semester examinations.
2. Student must maintain at least 80% attendance.
3. Students meeting these criteria receive a 5% scholarship on Academic Fee across all courses, except Veterinary, Maritime, BPT, Nursing, B.Pharma, and BCC.
