Admission Process

The university admission process begins with a single online application. Students should complete the application form, upload the required documents, and submit the fee payment confirmation before the published deadline.

Admissions are reviewed in stages. After the application window closes, the admissions office verifies eligibility, checks transcripts, and confirms the applicant's academic credentials.

Applicants receive an email notification when their application is accepted or if additional information is required. Accepted students must then complete the enrollment steps and confirm their course registration.

New students should also review orientation and registration instructions, including how to activate student accounts and access campus services.
