Centurion University of Technology and Management (CUTM)
Admissions Process 2025-2026 / 2026

ENTRANCE EXAMINATION (CUEE):
- Candidates seeking admission to CUTM courses are required to appear for the Centurion University Entrance Examination (CUEE) conducted by the University.
- CUEE is an online proctored entrance exam for students aspiring to take admission in all university courses.
- The exam format is primarily Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ), designed to match national-level entrance exam standards.

HOW TO APPLY (ONLINE APPLICATION & PAYMENT):
Candidates apply online via the CUTM website (www.cutm.ac.in) under the CUEE 2026 tab or directly at admissions.cutm.ac.in:

- Step 1: Registration
  Register in CUEE 2026 through admissions.cutm.ac.in by providing basic details and paying the application fee online. On successful submission, an Application ID and confirmation email are generated and sent to the applicant's registered email ID.

- Step 2: Entrance Examination (CUEE)
  CUEE is conducted according to scheduled dates displayed on the official website (www.cutm.ac.in) or communicated via registered email/mobile 1 week before the test. Applicants take CUEE 2026 online via proctored mode.

- Step 3: Provisional Admission & Fee Payment
  Successful/qualified applicants are contacted or may take provisional admission by depositing the minimum fee via UPI/Online/Card/DD at the university campus or paying online to the specified university account.
  * Balasore Campus Payment: https://rzp.io/rzp/CUTMBLSAdmission2026
  * Bhubaneswar Campus Payment: https://rzp.io/rzp/CUTMBBSR-Admission2026
  * Paralakhemundi Campus Payment: https://rzp.io/rzp/JITMPKD-Admissionfee2026

- Step 4: Document Verification & Final Registration
  For final admission, applicants must present the Original & 4 photocopies of required documents for verification: qualifying marksheet, pass certificate, rank/scorecard, Aadhaar card, PAN card, payment receipt, and photographs. Applicants pay fees as mentioned in their provisional admission letter before final registration.

ADMISSION CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY:
- Centurion University strictly follows the cancellation and refund policy issued by the University Grants Commission (UGC) for the relevant academic year. All cancellations and fee refunds are executed as per declared UGC policy.
